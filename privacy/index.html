Privacy

imessage.fun processes the phone number you message from, the messages you send, and the token details you provide so we can deliver the launch service, keep the service secure, and support your requests.

Message and launch information

Your carrier or messaging provider may retain message-routing records under its own policies. We may retain operational records for security, troubleshooting, abuse prevention, and service reliability.

Public blockchain data

Information included in a completed token launch may become public and permanent on a blockchain. This can include token metadata, wallet addresses, and transaction information. Public blockchain data cannot be made private after it is published.

What we will never ask for

imessage.fun will never ask you to text a wallet seed phrase or private key. Do not send either one through SMS.

Questions

For service updates or contact information, visit @imessagefun on X.
