Privacy

Last updated: September 12, 2026.

What the service processes

To process a launch request, the service may receive your phone number, message contents, the X handle you provide, public profile information returned by X, and blockchain transaction data. SMS delivery providers and infrastructure providers may process related metadata under their own terms.

Why it is used

Information is used to create and retain a short-lived launch draft, authenticate the confirmation flow, submit the requested blockchain transaction, return its status, prevent abuse, and operate the service.

Retention

Pending launch drafts are designed to expire after 15 minutes. Infrastructure, telecommunications and blockchain systems may retain logs or public transaction data independently. Onchain data is public and cannot generally be deleted by this service.
