The conversation

You'll be guided through every field. A typical launch looks like this:

YOU LAUNCH LAUNCH/SMS What's the token name? YOU Orbit LAUNCH/SMS Ticker? YOU ORBIT ...answer the remaining prompts... LAUNCH/SMS Here's your final review. Reply CONFIRM to launch. YOU CONFIRM LAUNCH/SMS You're live ✓

Before you confirm

Your final review is the moment to catch mistakes. Check these carefully:

  • The token name and ticker are spelled exactly how you want them.
  • Your logo and links point to the right places.
  • Your creator wallet is the address you control.
  • Your creator fee and buyback preference are correct.
Important: blockchain launches can be irreversible. If anything looks wrong, reply BACK before you confirm.

FAQ

Do I need a special app?

No. You use your normal SMS/Messages app.

Do I need an X account?

No. Social links are optional. You provide the token details directly.

Do I need a wallet app open?

No. You do need a valid EVM address if you want to receive creator fees.

Can I change something before launch?

Yes. Reply BACK before the final confirmation.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Reply CANCEL at any point before the launch completes.

Can I use it outside the US?

If your carrier can send SMS to the launch number, yes. Standard or international messaging charges may apply.

What network does it launch on?

Robinhood Chain.

Safety

  • Always verify the creator wallet before confirming.
  • Never text a seed phrase or private key. LAUNCH/SMS will never ask for one.
  • Only reply CONFIRM when the final review is correct.
  • Token launches can be irreversible and involve financial risk.
  • Service availability can depend on carrier, network and launch conditions.
