↗LAUNCH/SMS

Privacy

LAUNCH/SMS processes inbound phone numbers and launch metadata only to operate the requested launch flow. Conversation state is stored temporarily in the configured Redis backend. Your SMS carrier/provider may separately retain routing and message records under its own policy.

Wallet information

Creator fee addresses and launched token metadata are intentionally public once submitted onchain. A blockchain transaction cannot be made private after broadcast.

Operational secrets

SMS provider credentials, Redis credentials and launcher private keys are server-side environment variables and must never be exposed to browser code.

Testing

The browser tester uses a random local session identifier and forces dry-run mode. It cannot broadcast a token launch.
